arfjdmsAbout

Always curious. Usually building.

Developer, systems enthusiast, and the person behind arfjdms.

I like making things and knowing how they work.

I’m a developer who enjoys working across the whole stack. Some days that means building a web interface. Other days it means rendering pixels in C++, writing a Minecraft plugin, or figuring out how to run a model across a cluster.

I’m currently building Illatio, a self-hosted inference platform with a Go manager, an operator CLI, and a web console. It brings together the parts I enjoy most: systems, infrastructure, and making complicated things understandable.

Along the way, I’ve built open-source tools like Image Viewer and EaglerXSkinBridge. I’m happiest when a project gives me something new to learn and something useful to ship.
