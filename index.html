II

16,384 bytes in. One skin out.

An EaglercraftX client sends a player’s skin as raw ABGR8 bytes. Java Edition only takes a PNG. EaglerXSkinBridge converts one into the other, and this is its loop, ported line for line, running on a sample skin.

As the page scrolls, the bytes on the left are read four at a time, repacked from ABGR to ARGB, and painted into a 64 by 64 texture. The finished texture is a hooded figure with ember eyes and drawstrings, and its front faces then assemble into the standing figure.
